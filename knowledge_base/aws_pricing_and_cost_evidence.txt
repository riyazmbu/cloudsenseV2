AWS Cost Evidence and Pricing Boundaries

CloudSense separates observed spend from estimated optimization savings.

Observed spend should come from uploaded billing data or AWS Cost Explorer/resource-level billing where available. Cost Explorer and billing data may have different granularity and availability limits; resource-level savings should not be inferred when the resource ID is absent.

Optimization percentages in CloudSense are estimates used for prioritization. They are not AWS price quotes. A final savings figure should be validated against the actual AWS pricing for the target region, purchase option, architecture and workload.

For production recommendations, validate peak workload, dependencies, availability requirements, backup and retention requirements, monitoring/security agents, and rollback procedures before implementation.
