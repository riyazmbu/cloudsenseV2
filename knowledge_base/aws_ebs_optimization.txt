CloudSense AWS Knowledge Base — EBS Optimization

Unattached or orphaned EBS volumes continue to incur storage charges. AWS recommends reviewing unattached volumes regularly and validating dependencies before deletion.

An unattached volume should be considered a CLEANUP candidate, not an EC2-style CPU right-sizing candidate.

Before deletion: check volume age, tags, owner, dependencies, backup/retention requirements, compliance requirements, and whether a snapshot is needed. AWS notes that deleting an EBS volume removes its data; a snapshot can be used to preserve recoverability.

CloudSense wording: “Review unattached EBS volume for cleanup” rather than “delete volume”. Human approval is required.
